Before we start

NEXUS can learn your patterns over time — how you phrase things, what you tend to ask for, what "ambiguous" means for you specifically — to cut down back-and-forth and get more precise over time. This uses RFR2's relational/interaction modules against your real conversation data, stored as decaying hypotheses (never hard facts, never shared outside this system).

This is off by default. You can change this later from the ledger viewer. Nothing about you is modeled this way unless you say yes here.
